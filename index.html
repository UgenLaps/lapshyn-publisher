Lapshyn Publisher

Lapshyn Publisher is a private tool used by one family video creator, Eugene Lapshyn, to publish his own short videos.

When a video is finished and the creator has reviewed and approved it, the tool uploads the file from his own computer to his own accounts on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube (the Lapshyn Family channel and his other channels). Nothing is posted without his approval.

The tool is not offered to the public. It does not let other people sign in, and it does not read, collect or store data about other users.
